# Story ideas

Candidate premises and recurring threads. All of these are proposals; only the pilot is written.

## Five genre pillars

| Pillar | Story engine | Example title |
|---|---|---|
| **Reality Glitches** | Tiny contradictions gradually expose a broken world | *Every Tuesday, My Wife Forgets We Have a Son* |
| **Cosmic Weirdness** | Existential discoveries about space, time, or consciousness | *The Astronaut Who Found a Door at the Edge of the Universe* |
| **Impossible Places** | Buildings, hotels, and towns with bizarre rules | *The Hotel Where Nobody Is Allowed to Check Out on a Wednesday* |
| **Psychological Mysteries** | Neither protagonist nor listener can trust perception or memory | *I Received a Phone Call From Myself. He Told Me Not to Answer* |
| **Beautifully Absurd** | Impossible premises treated with dry humour and real emotion | *The Man Who Accidentally Purchased Yesterday* |

## Possible first release slate

1. **The Town Where Everyone Disappears for Eleven Minutes.** At 3:17 AM a whole town vanishes; one man stays awake to learn where they go.
2. **I Found a YouTube Channel Broadcasting Tomorrow.** Videos predict future events, until the protagonist sees themself.
3. **The Last Human in a Universe That Forgot Humanity.** Earth remains, but human history has been erased.
4. **My Neighbor Has Been Walking Into the Same Room for 40 Years.** A neighbouring house holds an impossible interior.
5. **The Man Who Remembered Other People's Futures.** Someone recalls future events that belong to strangers.
6. **The Universe Has a Customer Support Department.** Written as the pilot; see [Episode 001](../../episodes/001-universe-customer-support/README.md).

## The Broken Laws: a physics-inspired collection

Fictional extrapolations, not accounts of established physics. See [Science in the stories](science.md) before writing one.

| Title | Premise | Inspired by |
|---|---|---|
| **The Day Entropy Reversed** | Objects reassemble themselves; causality and memory start behaving strangely | The thermodynamic arrow of time |
| **The Man Who Refused to Collapse** | A physicist seems to remain in incompatible outcomes, and his versions disagree about which exists | Superposition and the measurement problem |
| **The Last Second of the Universe** | Near a black hole, an astronaut receives messages from epochs not yet lived | Relativity and time dilation |
| **The Universe That Forgot to Branch** | Memories from alternate lives appear as realities seem to merge | The Many-Worlds interpretation |
| **The Speed of Light Has Changed** | A tiny change to a constant destabilises ordinary physics | Relativity and causal structure |
| **The Smallest Possible Distance** | A researcher probes past known scales and finds a strange boundary to space | Quantum gravity and the Planck scale |

## Optional shared universe: Continuity Services

A background mythology could connect standalone stories without requiring anyone to listen in order. It's an **option, not established canon**.

Possible recurring clues:
- a Continuity Services business card found in an impossible hotel;
- a maintenance sticker on an asteroid or a telescope;
- the same original piano melody on an abandoned radio;
- the time 7:14, if carefully justified;
- a mention of Universal Maintenance in an unrelated reality-glitch story.

Keep clues rare and unobtrusive. Every episode must satisfy on its own. Before adding many connections, start a story bible that separates deliberate continuity from accidental reuse.
