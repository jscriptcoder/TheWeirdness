# Brand and visuals

Channel voice, copy drafts, and art direction. Everything here is a draft or proposal unless marked otherwise; nothing has final sign-off.

## Brand voice

- Curious, thoughtful, intimate, lightly mischievous.
- Dark and mysterious, but not relentlessly bleak.
- Welcomes intellectual curiosity without giving the audience homework.
- Never over-explains the central mystery mid-story.
- Humour comes from mundane human routines colliding with cosmic absurdity.

**Brand lines** (proposals; none chosen as final):

## Intro and outro

- **Intro:** the story's hook comes first, within 0–5 seconds. A short ident or "Welcome to The Weirdness. Reality is optional here." may follow *after* the opening intrigue, or be skipped. Never lead with branding.
- **Outro:** let the final line breathe. If "The Science Behind the Weirdness" is included, mark it as optional and place it after the story's ending.

## Art direction

**Keywords:** cosmic noir, elegant surrealism, impossible architecture, infinite corridors, doors to nowhere, monolithic shapes, cosmic eyes, eclipses, clocks, solitary silhouettes, dark teal, inky black, warm gold and amber portals, mist, minimal typography.

Avoid cluttered thumbnails, small detail, screaming faces, gore, and lots of small text.

## Thumbnails and video

- **Episode 001 thumbnail idea:** a phone on a desk in an ordinary office floating in space, with Earth or a nebula beyond the window. A small sign might read UNIVERSE DISCONTINUED, but the image must work without readable text.
- **Video:** start with 8–12 atmospheric scenes, very slow camera motion (Ken Burns or 2.5D), subtle transitions, minimal animation. The video adds mood; the audio tells the story on its own.
